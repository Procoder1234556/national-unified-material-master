## Card Styling Rules

- Background: White
- Border: 1px solid `#E5E2DE`
- Border radius: 12px
- Padding: 32px
- Box shadow: very soft
- Icon size: 40px
- Title: 20px, font-weight 700, margin-bottom 12px
- Body: 15px, line-height 1.6, color `#4A4A4A`

## Effects

- Cards appear with fade + upward movement when section enters view (stagger them by 0.1s each)
- On hover: card moves up 6px and shadow becomes stronger

## Image Recommendation

- Use simple icons only (no large images inside cards)
- Icon color must be `#E94344`

## Mobile

- Stack the three cards vertically with 20px gap between them
